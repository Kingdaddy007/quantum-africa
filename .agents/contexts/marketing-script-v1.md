## Diagnosis of the existing HyperFrames film

- Its seven-second opening has attractive schematic property imagery, but “Before the next move” does not establish a buyer's specific question as quickly as this script could.
- “Place it. Describe it.” and “See the range. Ask better.” describe stages more than explaining what the viewer gains from an estimated range.
- Similar six-to-seven-second scene durations and repeated lens/iris reveals give different ideas similar weight. Faster hooks, a held result explanation and a purposeful close would create more variation.
- The buyer/investor/agent segment consumes time without demonstrating a distinct use case. The newly confirmed client audience is buyers, realtors and companies.
- Schematic property imagery and a proposed interface make this a concept presentation. They do not supply evidence of valuation accuracy.
- Rendering worked in both engines. Engine capability is not a substitute for message specificity, art direction, editing and readable proof. The original Remotion source remains the recommended starting engine for the next cut because the user prefers that development direction; this is a project choice, not a universal claim of superiority.
