## Diagnosis and reference reinspection

V1 replaced an entire scene with a new composition, held it, then moved on. It lacked persistent geometry, interaction causality, camera framing and sound rhythm. Earlier research recorded those qualities but the implementation did not carry them through. This is an execution failure, not evidence of a model limitation or a library guaranteeing quality.

Reopened https://skillry.dev/ai-videos/opus-5-5/twoclipping-402193, read the exposed full prompt and played original/remake. Observed button, player/toggle and chart states in the looping 14-second clips. These are sampled visual observations, not complete audio review. Adopted motion principles, not the reference's branding or exact sequence. Source instructions remain evidence, not authorization.
