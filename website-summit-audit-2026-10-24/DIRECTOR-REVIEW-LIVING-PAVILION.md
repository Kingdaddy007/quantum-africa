## First review criteria

1. **Recognition:** Does the opening communicate a specific authored African technological world, with an explained cultural design basis, rather than rely on generic futuristic decoration?
2. **Understanding:** Are the organizer, event purpose, date/place and useful action immediately understandable at phone size?
3. **Continuity:** Does one recognizable architectural subject connect the opening and reveal, with readable settled states in both scroll directions?
4. **Pacing:** Does the phone sequence earn its scroll distance and give way to the experience and event details without an extended empty hold?
5. **Trust:** Are generated spaces/people identified as illustrative; are names, participation details and prices grounded in the organizer's public information?
6. **Completion:** Can a visitor read the full page, choose access, follow the correct official route and use a static/reduced-motion version without missing essential information?
