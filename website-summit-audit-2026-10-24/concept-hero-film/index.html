When and where is the Summit? +

Saturday, 28 November 2026, starting at 11:00 AM WAT in Lagos, Nigeria. The specific venue is to be communicated to registered participants.

What does my ticket include? +

Standard is $50, with refreshments, a souvenir, networking and VR games. VIP is $200, with an exclusive dinner with delegates listed by the organiser. Check final inclusions on the official registration page.

Is accommodation included? +

No. The published accommodation charges are separate: Standard $50 and VIP $100. Ask the team about the property, length of stay and availability.

Where can I check ticket availability and policies? +

Use the official Selar registration page for current availability and purchase terms. Contact the organisers if you need clarification on changes or refunds before booking.
