# Networking and panels — review experiment

Objective: after meeting the speakers, show how a visitor can participate through networking and panel discussions. User authorised the next section and review, not a redesign of accepted hero/VR/robot/speakers or completion of all remaining programme/commercial sections. Reversible local HTML/CSS/JS/assets only; no dependencies, publication or account actions.

Visual thesis: an ink-blue photographic room with confident live typography; a lateral editorial reveal moves from a small conversation to the wider panel discussion.

Content: one clear chapter heading, two distinct networking/panel beats, direct controls and an exit to workshops. Generated fictional people and venue are explicitly illustrative; no extra confirmed event claims.

Interaction thesis: slow, bounded image crop drift; the panel photograph wipes across the same room-sized canvas; readable caption holds replace one another. This is 2D photographic choreography, not invented 3D room geometry. No audio, video download, perpetual animation or forced progress.

## Storyboard

Controlling argument: the event is a place to meet people and exchange perspectives. Intended feeling curve: welcome → engagement → participation. Peak: panel reveal, quieter than the preceding 3D/portrait chapters. End: clear onward link to workshops.
