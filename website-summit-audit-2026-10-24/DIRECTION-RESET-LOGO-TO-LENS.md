## Recommended first experiment: the mark opens the experience

This is a proposed direction, not a final approved design. Build/test only the opening and first VR handoff before extending its visual grammar to the whole page.

**Opening:** Use the actual silver mark's roof facets and vertical blades as the basis of a brief opening mask. A faint glimpse of the underlying scene appears between the blades; the blades separate and the aperture grows to the viewport. The real mark resolves intact into the navigation. No loading percentage, obligatory wait, looping intro or audio dependency. Immediate navigation and a direct still equivalent must be available.

**Hero:** An original full-bleed, portrait-composed cinematic image/short scene connecting contemporary African people, real-estate space and technology. Human agency and a credible cultural/contextual basis matter more than a generic future skyline. No Lego-scale model, transplanted fantasy city, anonymous robot army or brown wash. Trial palette: ink/charcoal, silver, electric cyan, deliberate white type; natural skin/material colors come from the imagery. Keep clear event identity and a concise category descriptor. Put date/city/time into one readable information band by the primary action rather than isolated little labels at the top corners. Test typography with stronger display weight and less cramped tracking.

**Hero-to-VR transition:** A short reversible scroll sequence changes the image framing into a single lens-like aperture, then expands that view to fill the screen. A foreground frame and independently moving background provide credible limited depth. The settled state clearly reads as an invitation to a VR property experience. Avoid a literal two-eye split on a narrow phone, exaggerated distortion, fabricated headset telemetry, or a static image move that pretends to reveal unseen geometry. Existing footage may support a motion test; the final hero asset must be judged separately.

## Following sections: proposed experience roles

- Robotics/AI: a close encounter; a hand, face or machine moves into frame while a restrained scan/reveal exposes the scene. Preserve a visible programme label and avoid implied confirmed hardware.
- VR-exclusive formats: reveal property, VR cinema and games as three identifiable experiences within the lens sequence or its immediate continuation.
- Networking/panels: shared human scale; image panels and dialogue-oriented typography create the feeling of joining a conversation.
- Keynotes: portrait/stage composition and a deliberate title/role reveal; no fabricated quote or actual-event footage claim.
- Workshops: material/action/detail sequence showing participation, with a readable hands-on takeaway.
- Strategic partnerships: people/organisations coming into relation, followed by a specific supported invitation; no manufactured commercial outcomes.
- Speakers: real portrait montage with a clear settled lineup, names and roles always recoverable; tap/reveal and keyboard behavior must work without hovering.
- Attendance: full Standard/VIP comparisons; visible inclusions and cost, distinct optional accommodation, direct official registration route.
- Sponsorship: readable tiers and published benefits, with a cinematic entrance that finishes before detailed reading. Unresolved equity terms remain a distinct factual limitation.
- Close/footer: preceding content uncovers a persistent final invitation; short phone reveal and ordinary static layout when motion is reduced. Preserve real contact paths and exact 'Powered by Quantum Africa.'
