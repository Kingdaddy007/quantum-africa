## Directional judgment

The study now provides an intelligible experiment for the user's requested logo opening and immersive transition. The phone portrait protects the subject's face/headset better than a forced landscape crop. The property view has a coherent transition through a single lens and a readable settled invitation. The page palette uses ink/silver/cyan rather than the rejected mineral surface treatment.

The current movement is an animated real-logo treatment, masks and limited motion around illustrative stills. It is not native filmed/generated video, real interactive VR, an actual Summit venue, verified geography or documentary event footage. The cultural/creative fit remains for the user to judge; visible Black people and an imagined setting alone do not establish that the design fully answers the African identity brief.

The complete programme, speakers, commercial comparison and footer redesign are outside this first study. The next decision is whether this opening and lens direction earns further development.
